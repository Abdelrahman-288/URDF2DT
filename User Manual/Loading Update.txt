URDF2DT loading update — build d447b89

Open URDF now shows validation and geometry progress with a Cancel button.
Failed or cancelled loads preserve or restore the previous usable robot.
Missing meshes show notices; use Model & Appearance > Geometry to locate assets.

Cancellation during a mesh read takes effect after that individual read finishes.
A cancelled background validation may briefly need to finish before another load.
