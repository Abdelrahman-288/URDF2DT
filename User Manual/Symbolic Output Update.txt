Symbolic equations
------------------
Generate Symbolic FK or Symbolic Jacobian. Choose Matrix/Definitions for compact
form, or Expanded equations (no aliases) to read the underlying formulas.

Click a matrix cell. The selected-entry inspector lets you switch between compact
and expanded text, copy its equation, or copy expanded LaTeX. Constant cells can be
inspected too. The full matrix window offers the same controls.

Export publication LaTeX writes a complete document with frame context, joint
definitions and compact or expanded equations. Compile it with LuaLaTeX; required
packages include fontspec and breqn. Review equation line breaks for your paper.
No PDF typesetter is included with URDF2DT.

Expansion removes reusable aliases; it does not apply arbitrary simplification,
round small terms to zero or replace stored constants with guessed fractions.
Large expressions can reach the expansion limit; use compact output in that case.
Generation remains on demand and cancellable. Numerical results and model
precision are unchanged.
